## The product in one sentence

> Film a practice session. The app watches it like a coach and tells you exactly
> what to fix — specific to your technique, at the exact moment it went wrong.

## The differentiator in two sentences

Every finding cites a timestamp and a measured value; the app drops any claim it
cannot support with a measurement. In its advanced mode, the investigation also
adapts to what it finds — rather than running the same fixed checks every session.

## The Huawei angle in one sentence

A useful app with daily real use creates recurring inference and training demand
that Ascend is built to serve.
