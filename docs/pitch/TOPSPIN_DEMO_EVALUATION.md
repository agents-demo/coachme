# AI APP & SW Innovation Lab | Pnueli Lab | DEMO 1 — Topspin Review

| # | Evaluation Question | Response / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Demo Name** | **Topspin Review — an AI coaching team for racket sports.** Today: table-tennis video analysis. Vision: a multimodal practice companion for players, coaches and clubs. |
| 2 | **Short Description** | Upload a practice clip and receive a structured coaching report: measured body mechanics, ball/movement signals, timestamped findings, drills, focus and progress over time. The hook: the system shows *what it measured* and why a finding is supported.<br><br>**The differentiator:** a second, experimental **Investigator** mode where the model is the orchestrator. Instead of following a fixed measure-then-write pipeline, it chooses tools and time windows, may ignore ball tracking if irrelevant, or dig into wrist/racket mechanics because the evidence led there. Every conclusion must cite evidence returned by a tool. |
| 3 | **Problem Statement / Customer Bottleneck** | **1. Coaching is scarce and expensive.** A good coach costs USD 60–120/hour; many players train several times a week with little feedback between lessons.<br><br>**2. Video review is manual.** Players already film themselves, but reviewing clips, finding the right moment and knowing what to look for is slow and requires expertise.<br><br>**3. Generic AI is not trustworthy for technique.** Chat-based advice is not grounded in the player's actual movement, and most tools either show raw metrics or produce generic tips. Topspin links every finding to measured evidence and a timestamp.<br><br>**4. Progress is hard to see.** A single report is a novelty; repeated sessions, recurring issues and drills are what make improvement visible. |
| 4 | **Target Audience / Users** | **Primary B2C:** serious amateur table-tennis players (roughly 18–45, playing 3–5 times/week) and parents of junior players.<br><br>**Complementary B2B2C:** coaches, academies, clubs and schools that want to save review time and standardise feedback.<br><br>**Beachhead:** table tennis in English- and Chinese-speaking markets. Later: tennis, badminton, padel and squash. |
| 5 | **Value of the Product** | Turns a phone video into a practice decision a player can trust: what to fix, which drill to do next, and whether the issue is improving. For coaches, it is leverage — less time reviewing, more consistent feedback. The product is deliberately **coach-compatible**, not a replacement for the coach. |
| 6 | **Market Size – How Large Is It?** | Racket sports have a large participation base: table tennis ~300M recreational players (ITTF estimate), tennis ~87M (ITF), badminton 200M+, padel ~30M, squash ~20M and pickleball ~50M in the US; these overlap and are not additive, but establish **500M+ racket-sport participants**.<br><br>There is no clean published category for "AI racket-sport coaching." Planning estimates:<br>• Core AI-assisted coaching software: **USD 1.2–2.6B in 2025**, rising to **USD 4.5–9B by 2030** under a 25–35% base case.<br>• Racket-sport subset: **USD 250–450M in 2025**, rising to **USD 1.0–1.8B by 2030**.<br>• Topspin's primary serviceable market: ~**40M serious table-tennis players**; 20–30M reachable with language, payment and smartphone access.<br><br>These are **planning ranges**, not audited market data. Sources: ITTF, ITF Global Tennis Report, BWF, FIP/Padel Intelligence, SFIA, Grand View Research and adjacent analyst reports. |
| 7 | **Related AI Domain** | Multimodal video understanding; pose estimation; ball/person tracking; sports analytics; LLM/VLM coaching; **agentic orchestration** (the model selects tools, windows and next investigations); evidence-grounded report generation; local-first inference. Planned: audio input (coach notes/racket sound), voice output, live feedback, on-device NPU acceleration and coach knowledge retrieval. |
| 8 | **Competitors – Main Competitors; If None, Why Is There Value?** | The market is split between specialist tools and generic AI:<br>• **SwingVision** — strong tennis CV and line calls; tennis-only.<br>• **Sportsbox AI / Onform** — deep 3D golf mechanics and coach workflows; golf-first.<br>• **HomeCourt** — engaging basketball tracking; not racket sports.<br>• **V1 Sports, Hudl/Veo, Coach's Eye, PlaySight** — coach/team review, capture hardware or venue systems; less automated consumer coaching.<br>• **Generic AI assistants** — broad language ability, but no reliable sport-specific measurement or evidence loop.<br>• **Local Chinese apps and hardware** — distribution and price, but fragmented analytics.<br><br>No reviewed player clearly owns the full racket-sport loop: measurement → plain-language coaching → drills → progress. Most competitors automate a fixed measurement or generate generic text. Topspin's potential differentiator is **model-directed, evidence-grounded investigation** — the model can decide what to examine, ignore irrelevant signals and cite tool evidence — combined with table-tennis depth, bilingual web/Android and local-first processing. Weaknesses: no iOS, accounts/sync, coach workspace, live mode or multi-sport validation yet. |
| 9 | **Potential Product** | Start with a **B2C practice companion** for table tennis: personal coaching reports, drills and progress. Add a **coach/club workspace** as a complementary B2B2C layer: player progress, share links and team oversight. Later, expose an API/white-label offering and hardware/NPU partnerships. The long-term opportunity is a racket-sport coaching platform — not a generic social network. |
| 10 | **Open-Source Strategy – Development & Community Roadmap** | *Proposed roadmap — future work, not shipped capabilities or committed dates.*<br><br>**Phase 1 — Foundation:** publish setup, architecture, evaluation clips and reproducible quality tests; keep deterministic mode as the reliable baseline; keep the model-directed Investigator mode experimental; document its evidence, tool and safety rules.<br><br>**Phase 2 — Useful mode and coach channel:** CLI/API/UI mode selection, cancellation and run-scoped storage; coach workspace, share links and club licences; iOS and sync after retention is proven.<br><br>**Phase 3 — Sharper coaching:** ball tracking, stroke segmentation, racket-angle proxy, audio notes, voice output and stronger multimodal evidence.<br><br>**Phase 4 — Live and multi-sport:** live feedback between points, match scoring, tennis/badminton/padel validation and on-device NPU optimisation.<br><br>**Phase 5 — Ecosystem:** coach knowledge packs, federation/club integrations, cited drills and community-contributed evaluation clips.<br><br>**Cross-cutting requirements:** local-first video, consent for minors, easy deletion, no training use without explicit opt-in, cost caps per session, honest limitations and human/coach review before any claim is treated as medical or injury advice. |
