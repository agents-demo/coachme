# AI APP & SW Innovation Lab | Pnueli Lab | DEMO 1 — CoachMe

| # | Evaluation Question | Response / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Demo Name** | **CoachMe — an AI coaching team for racket sports.** Today: table-tennis video analysis. Vision: a multimodal practice companion for players, coaches and clubs. |
| 2 | **Short Description** | Upload a practice clip. Get back a coaching report: what to fix, at which exact moment in the video, and which drill will correct it.<br><br>Most AI tools generate generic tips ("improve your footwork"). This app measures what it sees — body angles, movement range, contact timing — and ties every finding to a specific timestamp in the video. If the AI cannot support a claim with a measurement, it drops the claim. The player can verify every point by watching the video themselves.<br><br>The app can also adapt what it examines based on what it finds, rather than running the same fixed checks every time. This adaptive investigation mode is opt-in and currently being validated on real coaching data. |
| 3 | **Problem Statement / Customer Bottleneck** | **1. Coaching is scarce and expensive.** A good coach costs USD 60–120/hour; many players train several times a week with little feedback between lessons.<br><br>**2. Video review is manual.** Players already film themselves, but reviewing clips, finding the right moment and knowing what to look for is slow and requires expertise.<br><br>**3. Generic AI is not trustworthy for technique.** Chat-based advice is not grounded in the player's actual movement, and most tools either show raw metrics or produce generic tips. CoachMe links every finding to measured evidence and a timestamp.<br><br>**4. Progress is hard to see.** A single report is a novelty; repeated sessions, recurring issues and drills are what make improvement visible. |
| 4 | **Target Audience / Users** | **Players** (primary paying customer): serious amateur table-tennis players — 18–45, training 3–5 times a week — and parents of junior players. They already film themselves and want to know what to fix.<br><br>**Coaches and clubs** (growth multiplier): a player shows the report to their coach; the coach wants it for all students. One coach brings 10–20 active users. Coach and club subscriptions grow on top of consumer demand, not instead of it.<br><br>**Geography first:** China is the primary market — not a future expansion. Approximately 85 million people play table tennis daily at a club level in China; around 10 million compete seriously. Since 2021, Beijing's 双减 policy banned for-profit academic tutoring, redirecting massive parental spending toward sport. China's children's sports industry is now worth ¥400B+ and growing at 15%+/year — parents who were spending on maths tutors are now spending on table tennis coaches and looking for trackable, evidenced progress. For junior players, the buyer is the parent, not the child. English-speaking markets second. Later expansion: tennis, badminton, padel and squash. |
| 5 | **Value of the Product** | **For the player:** a phone video becomes a specific practice decision — what to fix, which drill, and whether it improved since last session. Not generic tips: every finding cites a timestamp and a measured value the player can check themselves.<br><br>**For the coach:** leverage. The app watches the practice sessions the coach cannot attend and surfaces patterns a coach would normally spot only after many sessions. The coach confirms or overrides; the AI does the watching, the coach does the coaching. The product is designed to make coaches more effective, not to replace them. |
| 6 | **Market Size – How Large Is It?** | **Primary market — China:** table tennis is a national sport with ~85M daily club players and ~10M competitive players. The 双减 policy (2021) created a structural demand shift: China's children's sports industry is now ¥400B+ (~USD 55B) and growing at 15%+/year, with the youth sports market valued at USD 3.95B in 2024 (11% CAGR). Parents are the buyers for junior players.<br><br>**Global racket-sport base:** table tennis ~300M recreational players (ITTF), tennis ~87M (ITF), badminton 200M+, padel ~35M (FIP 2025), squash ~20M, pickleball ~24M US / ~22M globally active. These overlap but establish a combined base of **500M+ racket-sport participants** globally.<br><br>Planning estimates for AI-assisted coaching software:<br>• Core market: **USD 1.2–2.6B in 2025**, rising to **USD 4.5–9B by 2030**<br>• Racket-sport subset: **USD 250–450M in 2025**, rising to **USD 1.0–1.8B by 2030**<br>• CoachMe's primary serviceable market: ~**40M serious table-tennis players** worldwide; 20–30M reachable with language, payment and smartphone access<br><br>These are **planning ranges**, not audited data. Sources: ITTF, ITF, BWF, FIP, Grand View Research. |
| 7 | **Related AI Domain** | Multimodal video analysis; body-pose estimation (measuring joint angles from video without sensors); ball and player tracking; AI coaching report generation; session-to-session progress comparison. In its advanced mode, the AI decides what to examine next based on what it finds — rather than following a fixed measurement script. Planned: voice coaching output, audio input (spoken notes, racket sound), live real-time feedback during play, and running video analysis on-device or in a private cloud for speed and privacy. Built on the openJiuwen agent SDK; designed to run on Huawei Ascend hardware at scale. |
| 8 | **Competitors – Main Competitors; If None, Why Is There Value?** | The market is split between specialist tools and generic AI:<br>• **SwingVision** — strong tennis/pickleball CV, line calls, stats, and now an AI Coach feature with coaching prescriptions and drills. Expanded to pickleball. Apple-only — no Android, which excludes ~2B Android users and the entire China market.<br>• **Sportsbox AI / Onform** — deep 3D golf mechanics and coach workflows; golf-first.<br>• **HomeCourt** — engaging basketball tracking; not racket sports.<br>• **V1 Sports, Hudl/Veo, Coach's Eye, PlaySight** — coach/team review, capture hardware or venue systems; less automated consumer coaching.<br>• **Generic AI assistants** — broad language ability, but no reliable sport-specific measurement or evidence loop.<br>• **PingPi (乒球AI)** — AI table tennis video analysis app on App Store and Android; biomechanics metrics; targets the same use case. Direct competitor in the China market.<br>• **ByteDance / Douyin** — massive distribution (700M+ DAU), growing sports AI capabilities; structural platform threat if they prioritise table tennis coaching.<br>• **Chinese hardware ecosystem** — DHS (红双喜), Sanwei (三维) and similar equipment makers with club and retail distribution; potential smart-equipment bundles.<br><br>No reviewed player clearly owns the full racket-sport loop: measurement → plain-language coaching → drills → progress. Most competitors automate a fixed measurement or generate generic text. CoachMe's differentiator is **evidence-backed adaptive coaching** — the pipeline measures before it writes, drops unsupported findings, and (in Investigator mode) adapts the investigation to what is found — combined with table-tennis depth, bilingual web/Android and local-first processing. Next milestones: iOS, accounts/sync, coach workspace, live mode and multi-sport validation. |
| 9 | **Potential Product** | Start with a **consumer practice companion** for table tennis: personal coaching reports, drills and progress tracking for individual players. Add a **coach and club workspace** as the second product layer, once consumer demand is proven: player dashboards, share links and team accounts. Later: API and white-label for hardware partners and federations.<br><br>**China entry sequence:** (1) WeChat MiniProgram — users upload clips inside WeChat, analysis runs on Tencent Cloud, structured coaching report returns; no app-store install required, spreads through coaching groups and parent communities; (2) Android apps certified for Huawei AppGallery and the top five Chinese stores with PIPL-compliant design and domestic hosting; (3) Huawei AppGallery partnership and Ascend co-marketing. Pricing in China: RMB 30–50/month, not a USD 10 conversion.<br><br>The long-term opportunity is a racket-sport coaching platform. Table tennis is the entry point because stroke mechanics are the clearest to measure and China is the strongest early market. |
| 10 | **Open-Source Strategy – Development & Community Roadmap** | *Proposed roadmap — future work, not shipped capabilities or committed dates.*<br><br>**Phase 1 — Foundation:** publish setup, architecture, evaluation clips and reproducible quality tests; keep Standard mode as the reliable baseline; keep the model-directed Investigator mode opt-in while it is benchmarked; document its evidence, tool and safety rules.<br><br>**Phase 2 — Useful mode and coach channel:** CLI/API/UI mode selection, cancellation and run-scoped storage; coach workspace, share links and club licences; iOS and sync after retention is proven.<br><br>**Phase 3 — Sharper coaching:** ball tracking, stroke segmentation, racket-angle proxy, audio notes, voice output and stronger multimodal evidence.<br><br>**Phase 4 — Live and multi-sport:** live feedback between points, match scoring, tennis/badminton/padel validation and Ascend edge/cloud optimisation.<br><br>**Phase 5 — Ecosystem:** coach knowledge packs, federation/club integrations, cited drills and community-contributed evaluation clips.<br><br>**Cross-cutting requirements:** local-first video, consent for minors, easy deletion, no training use without explicit opt-in, cost caps per session, clear limitations and human/coach review before any claim is treated as medical or injury advice. |
