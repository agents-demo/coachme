# Service Detection

**Status:** not started — stub
**Phase:** C11 — Table-tennis specific
**Effort:** M
**Depends on:** pose + ball segments (measure stage)

## Goal

Flag likely serves (toss, no prior contact, player at own end) so serve
technique, placement, and variety get dedicated coaching instead of being
treated as rally strokes.

## Acceptance

- Serves flagged with confidence.
- Degrades gracefully when the toss is out of frame (false positives noted).
