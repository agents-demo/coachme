# Privacy & Compliance

**Status:** not started — stub
**Phase:** 0.7 — Foundations
**Effort:** S–M
**Depends on:** nothing

## Goal

Be explicit that video of a person is sensitive: consent line on Analyze,
configurable data retention, auto-prune on startup, local-first as the
default.

## Acceptance

- Retention setting prunes old data automatically.
- Consent is recorded before analysis.
- No frames leave the machine unless sharing is explicitly on.
