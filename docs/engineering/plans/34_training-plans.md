**Status:** not started — stub
**Phase:** D3 — Platform
**Effort:** L
**Depends on:** report history; feedback loop (0.3)

## Goal

A weekly plan generated from report history: targeted drills, load, and a
progress review; the agent remembers past focuses and adapts.

## Acceptance

- "Plan my week" produces a plan that references past issues and foci.
- Drills are checkable; next week's plan adapts based on completions.
- Every plan item cites evidence from the session history.
